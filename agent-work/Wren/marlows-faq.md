# Marlow's Mobile Bike Repair: Frequently asked questions

## Where do you work?

Marlow's comes to you in a van. There is no shop. The service area is Fremantle, Cottesloe and Claremont only.

## When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 7:30 am to 3:00 pm. Closed Sunday, Monday and public holidays.

## How much does it cost?

- Call-out fee: A$35. It is charged even if the bike cannot be fixed. It is taken off the bill if you go ahead with a repair.
- Basic tune-up (brakes, gears, tyre pressure, chain oil): A$89
- Full service: A$189
- Puncture repair: A$25 per tyre

## How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are done the same day. Parts that must be ordered take 3 to 5 working days. Marlow's will text you the date.

## How can I pay?

By card or bank transfer. Cash is not accepted, and payment plans are not offered.

## Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Every repair has a 30 day guarantee on the work. The guarantee does not cover parts that were already worn out.

## What will Marlow's not repair?

Marlow's does not repair the motors or batteries of electric bikes. It also does not repair bikes with carbon frames.

## How do I book?

Text 0400 555 019 or use the form at marlowsbikes.example/book. There is no email address for bookings.
